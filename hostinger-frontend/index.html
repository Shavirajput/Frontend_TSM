What we find

Six leaks we see again and again.

None of them look broken. All of them cost money. Revenue enters the top of your funnel — and drips out through cracks nobody is watching.

01 · WHAT WE FIND

The Clarity Leak

Your headline sounds impressive but the visitor still can't tell what you do, who it's for, or why you.

02 · WHAT WE FIND

The Trust Leak

They understand the offer but don't believe the company. No proof, no specifics, no authority. Uncertainty kills action.

03 · WHAT WE FIND

The Conversion Leak

“Learn More” → generic page → another page → an eleven-field form. Nothing is broken. The journey is leaking intent.

04 · WHAT WE FIND

The Performance Leak

Visually impressive, technically expensive, and still making people wait. Technology in the way of the objective.

05 · WHAT WE FIND

The Qualification Leak

You generate leads — the wrong ones. Sales burns cycles on people who were never going to buy.

06 · WHAT WE FIND

The Business Leak

Sometimes the website isn't the problem. If it isn't the bottleneck, we'll tell you. That's part of the audit.
